How does AI-generated content affect brand reputation?

Short answer

It can help or harm. This edited book examines how generative AI can improve personalisation and engagement, and how biased or impersonal AI content can erode trust and provoke public backlash.

What the study did

The volume explores generative AI in marketing, customer service and public relations, and its effects on authenticity and reputation.

What it found

  • AI-generated content brings efficiency but also new reputational risks.
  • Brands that integrate AI skilfully can improve personalisation and engagement.

Why it matters

It is aimed at marketers and business leaders deciding how far to automate brand communication.

Limits

This is an edited collection, so contributions vary in method and scope.

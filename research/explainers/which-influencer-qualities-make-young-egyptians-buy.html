Which influencer qualities make young Egyptians want to buy?

Short answer

Similarity and trustworthiness mattered most. Among 633 young Egyptian social media users, followers who saw an influencer as similar to themselves and as trustworthy reported the strongest purchase intention. Entertaining and useful content also raised purchase intention.

What the study did

The study surveyed 633 social media users and used structural equation modelling to test how the value of an influencer's content and the influencer's credibility shape purchase intention.

What it found

  • Every content value dimension tested had a significant positive effect on purchase intention. Entertainment value (SEM estimate 0.150) and functional value (0.118) contributed most.
  • Perceived similarity (0.280) and trustworthiness (0.218) had the largest credibility effects.
  • Expertise and attractiveness also had significant effects, but smaller ones.

Why it matters

For campaigns aimed at young Egyptian consumers, an influencer who feels relatable and honest is likely to do more than one who is simply expert or attractive.

Limits

The evidence comes from a single survey of young adults in Egypt.

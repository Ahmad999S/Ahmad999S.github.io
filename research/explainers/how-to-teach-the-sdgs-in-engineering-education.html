How can engineering schools build the UN Sustainable Development Goals into teaching?

Short answer

Project-based learning, problem-based learning and interdisciplinary teamwork are the main methods in use worldwide. Their adoption is held back by crowded curricula, limited staff training and few assessment tools.

What the study did

The paper reviews published practice worldwide on integrating the SDGs into engineering education. It then considers how engineering course content, together with compulsory climate change education for non-engineers, can contribute to SDG 9, SDG 12 and SDG 13.

What it found

  • Project-based, problem-based and interdisciplinary team approaches are used to build sustainability competence.
  • Curriculum overload, lack of faculty training and a limited number of assessment instruments are the main obstacles.

Why it matters

Programme leaders planning sustainability content get a map of methods and of the barriers they are likely to meet.

Limits

The paper is a literature review, not an evaluation of a specific programme.

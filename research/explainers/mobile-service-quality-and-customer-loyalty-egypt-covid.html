What kept Egyptian mobile customers satisfied and loyal during the COVID-19 lockdown?

Short answer

Service quality across the board. Among 328 Vodafone Egypt customers, network quality, value-added services, pricing plans, staff competence, billing, customer service, convenience and personalisation all played a significant role in satisfaction.

What the study did

An online questionnaire was given to Vodafone Egypt customers, and structural equation modelling was used to test the effects of eight service quality dimensions and the role of trust between satisfaction and loyalty.

What it found

  • All eight service quality dimensions, including service personalisation, played a significant role in customer satisfaction.
  • The findings support existing models and theories of service quality.

Why it matters

Telecom managers can use these dimensions to plan how to protect satisfaction, trust and loyalty during a crisis.

Limits

The data come from customers of one operator during the lockdown period.

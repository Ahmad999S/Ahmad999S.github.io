How should brands choose an influencer fraud detection tool?

Short answer

Match the tool to the brand's size and needs. A comparison of 20 leading tools found three groups: enterprise AI platforms, specialised vetting tools and quick-scan options.

What the study did

A qualitative content analysis of 20 fraud detection tools, comparing platform features, pricing, methods and compliance, with comparative analytical mapping.

What it found

  • Influencer fraud takes the form of fake followers, bot-driven engagement and manipulated growth.
  • The tool market is mature and falls into enterprise AI solutions, specialised vetting tools and accessible quick-scan options.

Why it matters

Fake engagement damages brand reputation and campaign returns, so systematic vetting before signing an influencer is now a basic step.

Limits

The analysis compares published tool features rather than testing detection accuracy.

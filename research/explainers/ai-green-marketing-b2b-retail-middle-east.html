How are Middle Eastern retailers using AI for green B2B marketing?

Short answer

Case studies of Carrefour UAE and Al Harbi Group show AI being used to optimise supply chains, reduce environmental impact, support purchasing decisions with real-time data, and make green initiatives more transparent to business partners.

What the study did

A qualitative comparison of two case studies: Carrefour UAE, a retail leader, and Al Harbi Group, a distributor of eco-friendly products.

What it found

  • AI enables real-time data analysis for smarter purchasing decisions.
  • AI supports transparency in green initiatives and stakeholder engagement in sustainability efforts.

Why it matters

It offers concrete regional examples for B2B firms planning AI-supported sustainability marketing.

Limits

It is based on two qualitative case studies.

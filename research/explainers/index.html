Research explained

Short, plain-language answers drawn from published studies by Dr Ahmad Yahya Shaheen and co-authors. Each page links to the original publication.

2025 · Discover Sustainability

Can AI influencers build the same trust as human influencers?

In this study of TikTok users in Egypt and Jordan, AI influencers formed real emotional bonds with followers and were seen as credible. In some respects they did better than human influencers at building a sense of community and widening followers' networks. Human influencers reached similar results by a different route: storytelling depth and cultural resonance.

2025 · Innovative Marketing

Which influencer qualities make young Egyptians want to buy?

Similarity and trustworthiness mattered most. Among 633 young Egyptian social media users, followers who saw an influencer as similar to themselves and as trustworthy reported the strongest purchase intention. Entertaining and useful content also raised purchase intention.

2026 · Scientific Reports

Why do Arab Gen Z consumers buy sustainable skincare?

Guilt about the environment is the link. Seeing yourself as a 'green' person raised purchase intention only through eco-guilt, not directly. Cultural values and green influencer marketing also shaped intention.

2026 · World Journal of Entrepreneurship, Management and Sustainable Development

Do AI shopping assistants make Gen Z trust online stores more, or worry more?

Both. When open-source AI recommenders (such as DeepSeek) were more personalised and responsive, Gen Z shoppers trusted them more, but they also became more concerned about privacy. Trust and privacy concerns were both linked to higher purchase intention.

2026 · International Journal of Interactive Mobile Technologies

Which Instagram Stories features drive engagement with brands?

Entertaining content and a trustworthy influencer had the strongest effects. In a survey of 443 active Instagram users in Jordan, all five Stories features tested raised engagement with brand content.

2025 · Sustainability (Switzerland)

Does renewable energy drive economic growth in Morocco?

Only in the short run, and the clearest effect was negative. Using data for 1993 to 2020, the study found a long-term equilibrium relationship among emissions, renewable energy, foreign direct investment, government green capital spending and growth. Green capital spending had a significant negative short-run correlation with growth. The long-run coefficients were not statistically significant.

2026 · Economies

Has public education spending driven economic growth in Egypt?

Not by volume alone. Over 1980 to 2023, physical capital was the strongest driver of long-run growth. Education spending showed a weakly negative long-run association, which the authors read as inefficient allocation rather than an absence of returns.

2025 · International Journal of Engineering Pedagogy

How can engineering schools build the UN Sustainable Development Goals into teaching?

Project-based learning, problem-based learning and interdisciplinary teamwork are the main methods in use worldwide. Their adoption is held back by crowded curricula, limited staff training and few assessment tools.

2026 · International Journal of Interactive Mobile Technologies

Is mobile learning good for students' digital well-being?

It cuts both ways. Mobile learning is flexible and engaging, but the reviewed studies also link it to distraction, anxiety, fatigue and shorter attention spans.

2026 · International Journal of Online and Biomedical Engineering

Can explainable AI help hospitals manage supply chain risk?

The study's prototype suggests it can. Tested on simulated scenarios, an explainable AI decision-support system identified high-risk supply situations, explained which factors drove the risk, and supported proactive mitigation.

2025 · Digital Horizons: Innovating Public Administration, Engineering, and Sustainable Business

Do cloud-based machine learning tools improve marketing results in Southeast Asia?

Most firms in the study say yes. Of 200 marketing professionals surveyed, 87% of businesses using cloud computing and machine learning reported improved marketing outcomes.

2025 · Digital Technologies and Transformations in Public Administration, Engineering, and Sustainable Business

How can AI make marketing more customer-centric without losing trust?

This chapter argues that AI tools such as machine learning, chatbots and data analytics can personalise marketing and improve engagement. To keep customer trust, firms need to address data privacy and stay transparent about how they use AI.

2025 · Transforming Business Practices With AI-Powered Green Marketing

How are Middle Eastern retailers using AI for green B2B marketing?

Case studies of Carrefour UAE and Al Harbi Group show AI being used to optimise supply chains, reduce environmental impact, support purchasing decisions with real-time data, and make green initiatives more transparent to business partners.

2026 · Strategies, Dynamics, and Societal Impacts of Digital Influencer Marketing

How should brands choose an influencer fraud detection tool?

Match the tool to the brand's size and needs. A comparison of 20 leading tools found three groups: enterprise AI platforms, specialised vetting tools and quick-scan options.

2026 · Strategies, Dynamics, and Societal Impacts of Digital Influencer Marketing

What does research say about the strategies and societal impacts of influencer marketing?

This edited book brings together research on the theory, strategy, psychology, ethics and social effects of digital influencer marketing.

2025 · Impacts of AI-Generated Content on Brand Reputation

How does AI-generated content affect brand reputation?

It can help or harm. This edited book examines how generative AI can improve personalisation and engagement, and how biased or impersonal AI content can erode trust and provoke public backlash.

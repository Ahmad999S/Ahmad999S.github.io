How can AI make marketing more customer-centric without losing trust?

Short answer

This chapter argues that AI tools such as machine learning, chatbots and data analytics can personalise marketing and improve engagement. To keep customer trust, firms need to address data privacy and stay transparent about how they use AI.

What the study did

The chapter examines how AI tools are used to personalise marketing, improve engagement and support sustainable business growth, and analyses the ethical challenges involved.

What it found

  • AI supports personalisation, engagement and sustainable growth.
  • Data privacy and customer trust are the central ethical challenges.

Why it matters

It gives managers a framework for adopting AI in marketing while keeping customer trust.

Limits

The chapter is conceptual and does not report new data.

Can explainable AI help hospitals manage supply chain risk?

Short answer

The study's prototype suggests it can. Tested on simulated scenarios, an explainable AI decision-support system identified high-risk supply situations, explained which factors drove the risk, and supported proactive mitigation.

What the study did

Following design science research, the authors designed an online decision-support system that combines supplier dependability, lead-time inconsistency, demand uncertainty and the importance of each medical supply in an interpretable AI framework. They evaluated it descriptively using simulated healthcare supply chain scenarios.

What it found

  • The system recognised high-risk supply situations and explained the factors behind them.
  • Explanations are intended to build trust and ease of use for hospital procurement staff.

Why it matters

Many AI risk tools are not adopted because procurement staff cannot see how they reach their conclusions. Explainability addresses that barrier directly.

Limits

The evaluation is descriptive and uses simulated scenarios rather than live hospital data.

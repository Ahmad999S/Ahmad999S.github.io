What kind of YouTube influencer content makes Egyptian followers want to buy?

Short answer

Content that entertains, is useful, or carries social value. In a survey of 749 respondents in Egypt, these three content values raised followers' purchase intention, while how informative the content was had no effect.

What the study did

The authors built a model from the theory of consumption values and advertising value theory, collected data through an online questionnaire, and analysed it with PLS-SEM.

What it found

  • Entertainment value, functional value and social value of influencer content affected followers' purchase intention.
  • Informativeness had no effect on purchase intention.

Why it matters

Brands working with YouTube influencers in Egypt should prioritise entertaining, useful and socially relevant content over purely informational messages.

Limits

The study surveyed followers of YouTube influencers in Egypt only.

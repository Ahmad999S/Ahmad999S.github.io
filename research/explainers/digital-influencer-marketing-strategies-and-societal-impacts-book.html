What does research say about the strategies and societal impacts of influencer marketing?

Short answer

This edited book brings together research on the theory, strategy, psychology, ethics and social effects of digital influencer marketing.

What the study did

The volume covers platform algorithms, audience engagement, trust-based relationships, influencer characteristics, psychological mechanisms of influence and ethics.

What it found

  • Topics include fraud detection, mental health, social media, generative AI, neuromarketing, and ethics and law.

Why it matters

It is aimed at business owners, marketers and researchers who need a broad view of how influencer marketing shapes consumption, identity and digital work.

Limits

This is an edited collection, so contributions vary in method and scope.

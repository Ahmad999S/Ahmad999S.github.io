Which Instagram Stories features drive engagement with brands?

Short answer

Entertaining content and a trustworthy influencer had the strongest effects. In a survey of 443 active Instagram users in Jordan, all five Stories features tested raised engagement with brand content.

What the study did

Active Instagram users in Jordan who regularly watch Stories and follow at least one brand completed an online survey. The authors tested measurement with confirmatory factor analysis and estimated the engagement model with structural equation modelling (CFI = 0.934, RMSEA = 0.044).

What it found

  • All five features raised cognitive processing (attention and thought about the brand): entertaining content (β = 0.321), influencer trustworthiness (β = 0.318), user-generated content (β = 0.200), up-to-date information (β = 0.171) and interactivity (β = 0.142).
  • Cognitive processing strongly predicted affection (β = 0.647) and activation (β = 0.718).
  • The five features explained a modest share of cognitive processing (R² = 0.241).

Why it matters

In a mobile-first Middle Eastern market, brands can treat entertainment and a credible presenter as their main levers in Stories.

Limits

The sample was 60.7% female and mostly aged 19 to 25, and the design is cross-sectional.

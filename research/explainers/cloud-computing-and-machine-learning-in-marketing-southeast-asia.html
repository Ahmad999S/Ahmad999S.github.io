Do cloud-based machine learning tools improve marketing results in Southeast Asia?

Short answer

Most firms in the study say yes. Of 200 marketing professionals surveyed, 87% of businesses using cloud computing and machine learning reported improved marketing outcomes.

What the study did

A quantitative survey of 200 marketing professionals in e-commerce, retail, telecommunications and other industries across Southeast Asia, examining customer segmentation, personalised marketing and campaign optimisation.

What it found

  • Better customer segmentation and personalised campaigns were the most notable benefits.
  • Data privacy concerns and a shortage of skilled professionals were the main barriers to wider adoption.

Why it matters

Firms considering these tools can expect gains in targeting, but should plan for privacy safeguards and skills development.

Limits

Results reflect self-reported outcomes from marketing professionals.
